Drag, pinch, use the mouse wheel, double-click or focus the map and use arrow and +/- keys. The controls at the right zoom, rotate and tilt (click the compass to face north again) and show your location. Click a marker for its popup. Right-drag, Ctrl-drag, two fingers or Shift+arrow keys rotate and tilt. Right-click or open Actions for map commands. Shift-drag to measure an area.
